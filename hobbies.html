Featured Games

Online Game

Counter-Strike 2

Counter-Strike 2 is my favorite online game to play on PC. It is a tactical first-person shooter where two teams, Terrorists and Counter-Terrorists, compete in objective-based rounds like bomb defusal and hostage rescue.

I enjoy playing CS2 with my friends because it requires quick reflexes, clear communication, and teamwork. Each round requires strategy, managing in-game economy, and coordinating smoke grenades and flashbangs to take control of key map areas.

Physical Game

Cricket

Cricket is the physical sport I play most frequently. It is an outdoor team sport played between two teams of eleven players each. I regularly play taped-ball and hard-ball cricket matches on weekends with my university friends.

I usually play as an all-rounder, contributing with both batting and medium-pace bowling. Playing cricket keeps me active and healthy, and it teaches team coordination, patience, and how to perform under match pressure.
